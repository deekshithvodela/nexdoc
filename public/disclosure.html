Data Disclosure

Last updated: July 2026

NexDoc is designed to aggregate and simplify complex medical counseling datasets. All seat counts, cutoff closing ranks, mapping statistics, and college directories shown are derived from public datasets.

1. Data Sources

The core data is sourced from public declarations and official documents published by government agencies:

  • National Medical Commission (NMC) (nmc.org.in): Officially approved MBBS seat counts, college inclusion directories, university affiliations, year of establishment, and NMC registration/recognition statuses.
  • Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) (mcc.nic.in): Official seat allotment matrices, counseling guidelines, and seat allocation cutoff closing ranks for Round 1, Round 2, Round 3, and stray vacancy allocation levels.

2. Interpretation & Mappings

NexDoc consolidates multiple discrete CSV/JSON tables into a unified search layout. The correlation of state-level code names to All India Quota (AIQ) college identification numbers has been mapped using historical data matching. While mapping accuracy is prioritized, minor code mapping variances may arise.

3. Disclaimer & Unofficial Status

NexDoc is an unofficial, independent educational dashboard. It is not affiliated, authorized, endorsed, or in any way officially associated with the NMC, the MCC, the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, or any state medical counseling authority. Aspirants must cross-reference actual numbers with current-year official portals and choice filling guidelines before finalizing submissions.
