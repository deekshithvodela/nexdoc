Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

NexDoc is committed to offering a premium, secure, and privacy-respecting explorer tool for medical aspirants. Our policy outlines the complete lifecycle of data used by the app.

1. Direct Data Collection

NexDoc does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). No sign-up, registration, email address, phone number, or credential input is required to access the NEET Seat Explorer dashboard.

2. Local Processing of User input

All tool activities (such as filter selections, region preferences, college comparison lists, and NEET closing ranks entered into the predictor) are processed entirely client-side inside your browser:

  • They remain in transient, non-persistent memory variables during active usage.
  • No user input or search details are transmitted to external servers, logging services, or tracking databases.

3. Offline Cache & PWA Capabilities

NexDoc implements a Progressive Web App (PWA) Service Worker (`sw.js`) and utilizes standard browser cache mechanisms. This is done exclusively to pre-cache the static markup files (HTML, CSS, JS) and local, read-only university allocation databases to enable rapid offline loading speeds. No tracking or telemetry scripts are placed in Cache Storage.

4. Advertising & Tracking Cookies

This web application is free from commercial third-party trackers, analytics pixels, advertising network cookies, and behavioral marketing SDKs.
